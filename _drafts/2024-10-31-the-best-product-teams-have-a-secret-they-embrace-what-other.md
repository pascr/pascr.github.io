The best product teams have a secret: they embrace what others run from.

Don't fear failures.
Transform them into strength.

Here's how great teams succeed:
- Every incident becomes a learning opportunity. 

They document solutions and put measures in place to prevent failure to happen again. Each setback makes them stronger.
- They stay united under pressure. 

The more brains in the game, the faster solutions emerge. Instead of pointing fingers, they focus purely on solutions. Blame doesn't ship productsaction does.
- They analyze and adapt. 

Post-mortems aren't optional. They dig deep into root causes, document lessons learned, and implement changes. Every crisis shapes their future success.

Turn every incident into progress.
Make every failure count.
