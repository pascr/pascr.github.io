- Did you know your company's org chart might be shaping your software architecture more than you realize? 

This is Conway's Law in Actionthe idea that systems mirror the communication structures within organizations.

As an engineering manager, I've seen this play out repeatedly.  For example, I've watched teams unintentionally duplicate efforts, resulting in parallel systems with similar purposesall because the teams didn't have enough cross-communication.
- Next time you design a system, examine your org chart. You might be surprised at how it influences your architecture!
