The more I know, the less creative I become.

Sounds backwards, right?

But expertise creates mental boxes. 
You learn what can't be done. What is impossible. What has been tried before.

Meanwhile, someone new walks in and breaks all your rules.
They ask the "naive" question that unlocks everything.

Great innovations come from curiosity, not certainty.
So, stay curious!
