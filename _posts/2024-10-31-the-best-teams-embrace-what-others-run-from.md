The best product teams have a secret: they embrace what others run from. They don't fear failures; they transform them into strength.

Here is how great teams succeed.

**Every incident becomes a learning opportunity.** They document solutions and put measures in place so the failure does not happen again. Each setback makes them stronger.

**They stay united under pressure.** The more brains in the game, the faster solutions emerge. Instead of pointing fingers, they focus purely on solutions. Blame doesn't ship products; action does.

**They analyze and adapt.** Post-mortems aren't optional. They dig deep into root causes, document lessons learned and implement changes. Every crisis shapes their future success.

Turn every incident into progress. Make every failure count.
