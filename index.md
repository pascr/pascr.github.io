For the past 15 years, I've built and led engineering teams that ship resilient, high-quality software at scale. Today at Visium, I'm focused on a harder version of that problem: building AI systems that regulated industries can actually trust.

Most AI systems don't make it past pilot in Life Sciences and other regulated settings, and for good reason. Probabilistic systems are difficult to validate, audit, and certify. My job, as CTO and Partner at Visium, is to close that gap: defining the technical strategy, leading the engineering org, and turning Agentic AI research into products that hold up under real compliance and operational constraints. Before Visium, I led engineering at Bestmile through to its acquisition by ZF Group.

What I care about: engineering cultures where people do their best work. Distributed systems that don't fall over at 3am. AI solutions that create real business value, not demos. I've also been mentoring the next generation of engineering leaders through PlatoHQ, The Mentoring Club, and MentorCruise since 2020.

If you're working on scaling an AI product into a regulated environment, restructuring an engineering org, or thinking through technical strategy for an AI-first company, I'm always happy to talk.
